RWKV-7 HuggingFace port -- ln_x GroupNorm epsilon, checked against the fp32 numpy oracle
=========================================================================================
2026-07-29, RTX 5090, lmsysorg/sglang:dev-cu12 container, fp32 both sides.

Weights: rwkv7-g1d-0.1b-20260129-ctx8192.pth (768 / 12 heads / head_dim 64) and its
converted HF copy. emb.weight verified bit-identical between the two, so the two sides
are the same model and not two checkpoints that resemble each other.

Reference: bench/oracle_numpy.py, which hardcodes the RWKV-7 reference's eps=64e-5.
Prompt: "\nThe Eiffel Tower is located in the city of"  (12 tokens), 32 greedy tokens.

  oracle greedy text:
    ' Paris, France. It is a tower that was built in 1889 and is one of the most
     recognizable landmarks in the world. The tower is made of steel'

  arm "head_dim (fixed)"  eps=6.4e-4   32/32 token-identical   max|logit delta| = 5e-05
  arm "num_heads (old)"   eps=1.2e-4   11/32, diverges at 9    max|logit delta| = 4.318
    old-arm text: ' Paris, France. It is a tower that stands on the site of the'

Second non-square width, same protocol, rwkv7-g1g-1.5b-20260526-ctx8192.pth converted
to HF by the port's own converter (2048 / 32 heads / head_dim 64), emb.weight again
verified bit-identical:

  oracle greedy text:
    ' Paris, France.\nThe Eiffel Tower is a symbol of Paris and France.\nThe Eiffel
     Tower is a popular tourist attraction.\nThe'

  arm "head_dim (fixed)"  eps=6.4e-4   32/32 token-identical   max|logit delta| = 3e-05
  arm "num_heads (old)"   eps=3.2e-4   30/32, diverges at 14   max|logit delta| = 0.527
    old-arm text: ' Paris, France.\nThe Eiffel Tower is a symbol of France and Paris'

Severity tracks the num_heads:head_dim ratio -- 5.33x at 0.1B gives 11/32 and 4.318,
2.00x at 1.5B gives 30/32 and 0.527. The 1.5B row is the more instructive one: a
swapped word order at 30/32 reads like sampling noise and would not survive eyeball
review as evidence of anything.

Why it mattered so much at this width: on trained weights the per-head variance going
into ln_x is tiny -- median 1.345e-06 at layer 0, min 4.2e-12, 84.4% of heads below the
epsilon itself -- so sqrt(var + eps) is essentially sqrt(eps) and the wrong constant
rescales the whole normalisation by sqrt(64/12) = 2.31x instead of perturbing it.

Same 0.1B, fp16, 512-token forward, the two epsilons against each other:
  max|logit delta| 2.797 on a magnitude of 48.03 (5.8% relative), mean 0.154,
  42 of 512 positions change their argmax, greedy agreement 18.75% over 64 tokens.

num_heads and head_dim coincide only at hidden_size 4096 (head_dim 64), which is the
7.2B -- the checkpoint every earlier accuracy measurement of the port used, and the one
width where this bug is invisible.
